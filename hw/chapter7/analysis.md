# Chapter 7 Analysis

## Q1
- Prediction / 预测: FIFO and SJF are expected to have an average response time of 200.00, an average turnaround time of 400.00, and an average wait time of 200.00. / 预计FIFO和SJF的平均响应时间为200.00，平均周转时间为400.00，平均等待时间为200.00。
- Reasoning / 理由: Since all three jobs require 200 units of CPU time, SJF has no reason to change their original order. Both algorithms execute the jobs in the same sequence. Their completion times are 200, 400, and 600, while their first-run times are 0, 200, and 400. / 由于三个作业都需要200个单位的CPU时间，SJF没有必要调整原有顺序，因此两种算法会按照相同的顺序执行。它们的完成时间分别为200、400和600，首次运行时间分别为0、200和400。
- Verified result / 验证结果: Both FIFO and SJF produce an average response time of 200.00, an average turnaround time of 400.00, and an average wait time of 200.00. / FIFO和SJF的平均响应时间均为200.00，平均周转时间均为400.00，平均等待时间均为200.00。
- Analysis / 分析: The simulation results agree with the prediction. When every job has the same length, SJF cannot prioritize any job over another, so its schedule is identical to FIFO. This indicates that SJF's advantage becomes apparent when job lengths are different. / 模拟结果与预测一致。当所有作业的长度都相同时，SJF无法将某个作业优先于其他作业，因此它的调度顺序与FIFO一致。这表明，当作业长度存在差异时，SJF的优势才更容易体现出来。

## Q2
- Prediction / 预测: For FIFO and SJF, the expected average response time is 133.33, the average turnaround time is 333.33, and the average wait time is 133.33. / 预计FIFO和SJF的平均响应时间为133.33，平均周转时间为333.33，平均等待时间为133.33。
- Reasoning / 理由: The jobs have lengths of 100, 200, and 300, which are already arranged from shortest to longest. Therefore, SJF will select them in the same order as FIFO. Their first-run times are 0, 100, and 300, and their completion times are 100, 300, and 600. / 三个作业的长度分别为100、200和300，提交顺序已经按照从短到长排列。因此，SJF会像FIFO一样依次执行它们。首次运行时间分别为0、100和300，完成时间分别为100、300和600。
- Verified result / 验证结果: Both FIFO and SJF show an average response time of 133.33, an average turnaround time of 333.33, and an average wait time of 133.33. / FIFO和SJF的平均响应时间均为133.33，平均周转时间均为333.33，平均等待时间均为133.33。
- Analysis / 分析: The predicted values match the simulation results. Since the jobs are already arranged in shortest-first order, the two scheduling algorithms generate the same execution sequence. Later jobs must wait for the earlier jobs to finish, so their response times depend on the accumulated lengths of the preceding jobs. / 预测值与模拟结果一致。由于作业本身已经按照从短到长的顺序排列，两种调度算法会产生相同的执行顺序。后续作业必须等待前面的作业完成，因此它们的响应时间取决于前面作业运行时间的累积。

## Q3
- Prediction / 预测: With RR using a quantum of 1 for jobs of lengths 100, 200, and 300, the expected average response time is 1.00, the average turnaround time is 465.67, and the average wait time is 265.67. / 对于长度分别为100、200和300的作业，当RR的时间片为1时，预计平均响应时间为1.00，平均周转时间为465.67，平均等待时间为265.67。
- Reasoning / 理由: The jobs receive their first CPU time at 0, 1, and 2, resulting in response times of 0, 1, and 2 and an average of 1.00. Job 0 completes at time 298. After that, jobs 1 and 2 alternate until job 1 finishes at 499, and job 2 completes at 600. The turnaround times are therefore 298, 499, and 600, averaging 465.67. Subtracting each job's length gives waiting times of 198, 299, and 300, with an average of 265.67. / 三个作业首次获得CPU的时间分别为0、1和2，因此响应时间分别为0、1和2，平均值为1.00。作业0在时间298完成。随后作业1和作业2交替运行，作业1在499完成，作业2在600完成。因此，周转时间分别为298、499和600，平均值为465.67。用周转时间减去各自的作业长度，可得等待时间198、299和300，平均值为265.67。
- Verified result / 验证结果: RR with quantum 1 reports an average response time of 1.00, an average turnaround time of 465.67, and an average wait time of 265.67. The individual turnaround times are 298, 499, and 600. / RR在时间片为1时，平均响应时间为1.00，平均周转时间为465.67，平均等待时间为265.67。各作业的周转时间分别为298、499和600。
- Analysis / 分析: The prediction agrees with the reported results. Compared with FIFO and SJF, which have an average response time of 133.33 and an average turnaround time of 333.33, RR significantly reduces response time but increases turnaround time. This illustrates the trade-off of RR: each job gets an early opportunity to use the CPU, but frequent rotation can delay overall completion. / 预测与输出结果一致。与平均响应时间为133.33、平均周转时间为333.33的FIFO和SJF相比，RR显著降低了响应时间，却增加了周转时间。这体现了RR的权衡：每个作业都能较早获得CPU，但频繁轮换可能延长整体完成时间。

## Q4
- Prediction / 预测: SJF and FIFO have identical turnaround times when all jobs arrive simultaneously and the submission order is already arranged by non-decreasing job length. This also applies when all jobs have equal lengths. / 当所有作业同时到达，且提交顺序已经按照作业长度从小到大排列时，SJF与FIFO的周转时间相同。所有作业长度相等时也符合这一条件。
- Reasoning / 理由: FIFO follows the submission order, whereas SJF selects the shortest available job first. If both rules result in the same sequence, every job finishes at the same time under either algorithm. However, if a long job is placed first, FIFO forces shorter jobs to wait behind it, creating the convoy effect. For example, with lengths 300, 200, and 100, FIFO should produce an average turnaround time of 466.67, while SJF should produce 333.33. / FIFO按照提交顺序执行，而SJF优先选择最短的可运行作业。如果两种规则产生相同的执行顺序，那么每个作业在两种算法下都会在相同时间完成。但是，如果较长的作业排在前面，FIFO就会使较短的作业等待它结束，从而产生护航效应。例如，作业长度为300、200和100时，FIFO的平均周转时间应为466.67，而SJF应为333.33。
- Verified result / 验证结果: For jobs of lengths 300, 200, and 100, FIFO produces an average turnaround time of 466.67 and an average response time of 266.67. SJF produces an average turnaround time of 333.33 and an average response time of 133.33. For the already sorted jobs in Q2, both algorithms produce an average turnaround time of 333.33. / 对于长度为300、200和100的作业，FIFO的平均周转时间为466.67，平均响应时间为266.67；SJF的平均周转时间为333.33，平均响应时间为133.33。对于Q2中已经按从短到长排列的作业，两种算法的平均周转时间均为333.33。
- Analysis / 分析: The simulation supports the prediction. When a long job appears first, FIFO makes all shorter jobs wait until it finishes, while SJF schedules the shorter jobs earlier. Consequently, their turnaround times differ. When the submission order already matches the shortest-first order, both algorithms produce the same result. / 模拟结果支持这一预测。当长作业排在最前面时，FIFO会使所有较短的作业都等待它完成，而SJF会优先安排较短的作业。因此，两者的周转时间会有所不同。如果提交顺序本来就符合从短到长的排列，两种算法便会产生相同的结果。

## Q5
- Prediction / 预测: SJF and RR can produce identical response times when jobs are submitted from shortest to longest and the quantum is at least as long as every job that must finish before the next job's first turn. A simple sufficient condition is that the quantum is no shorter than every job's length, so RR does not preempt any job. / 当作业按照从短到长的顺序提交，并且时间片足够长，使下一个作业首次运行前需要执行的作业都能在一个时间片内完成时，SJF与RR可以得到相同的响应时间。一个简单的充分条件是时间片不短于任何作业的长度，此时RR不会抢占作业。
- Reasoning / 理由: A job's response time is determined by the CPU time consumed before its first execution. Under SJF, this is the total length of the jobs scheduled before it. Under RR, the first round contributes up to one quantum for each preceding job. The two can therefore match when every preceding job completes within one quantum. For jobs of lengths 100, 200, and 300, a quantum of 200 should give RR an average response time of 133.33, matching SJF; a quantum of 100 should give 100.00. / 作业的响应时间由它首次运行之前CPU已执行的时间决定。对于SJF，这等于排在它前面的作业长度之和；对于RR，第一轮中前面的每个作业最多占用一个时间片。因此，如果前面的每个作业都能在一个时间片内完成，两者就可能得到相同的结果。对于长度为100、200和300的作业，时间片为200时，RR的平均响应时间预计为133.33，与SJF相同；时间片为100时，预计为100.00。
- Verified result / 验证结果: For jobs of lengths 100, 200, and 300, RR with quantum 200 has an average response time of 133.33, matching SJF. RR with quantum 100 has an average response time of 100.00, which differs from SJF. / 对于长度为100、200和300的作业，RR在时间片为200时的平均响应时间为133.33，与SJF相同；时间片为100时的平均响应时间为100.00，与SJF不同。
- Analysis / 分析: The results support the prediction. With a quantum of 200, the first two jobs, whose lengths are 100 and 200, finish within their respective turns, so the first-run times match those under SJF. With a quantum of 100, the second job is interrupted after its first time slice, allowing the third job to start earlier, at time 200 rather than 300. Consequently, RR has a lower average response time in this case. / 结果支持这一预测。时间片为200时，长度为100和200的前两个作业都能在各自的时间片内完成，因此它们的首次运行时间与SJF相同。时间片为100时，第二个作业在第一个时间片结束后被抢占，使第三个作业能够提前在时间200而不是300开始运行。因此，在这种情况下，RR的平均响应时间更低。

## Q6
- Prediction / 预测: As the lengths of all jobs increase proportionally, SJF's average response time also increases linearly. For jobs 10, 20, and 30, the expected average response time is 13.33; for jobs 100, 200, and 300, it is 133.33; and for jobs 1000, 2000, and 3000, it is 1333.33. / 当所有作业的长度按相同比例增加时，SJF的平均响应时间也会线性增加。作业长度为10、20、30时，预计平均响应时间为13.33；长度为100、200、300时，为133.33；长度为1000、2000、3000时，为1333.33。
- Reasoning / 理由: SJF is non-preemptive, meaning a job must wait for the jobs scheduled before it to finish. Its response time is the sum of the lengths of those preceding jobs. Multiplying every job length by ten therefore multiplies every response time by ten as well. / SJF属于非抢占式调度，作业必须等待排在它前面的作业完成后才能开始运行。因此，它的响应时间等于前面所有作业的运行时间之和。将所有作业长度扩大十倍，也会使每个作业的响应时间扩大十倍。
- Verified result / 验证结果: The measured average response times are 13.33 for jobs 10, 20, and 30; 133.33 for jobs 100, 200, and 300; and 1333.33 for jobs 1000, 2000, and 3000. / 作业长度为10、20、30时，平均响应时间为13.33；长度为100、200、300时，为133.33；长度为1000、2000、3000时，为1333.33。
- Analysis / 分析: The simulation confirms a linear relationship: increasing all job lengths by a factor of ten also increases the average response time by a factor of ten. This reveals a limitation of SJF for response time, because jobs scheduled later must wait for all preceding jobs to finish. / 模拟结果证实了线性关系：所有作业长度扩大十倍后，平均响应时间也扩大十倍。这反映了SJF在响应时间方面的局限性，因为排在后面的作业必须等待前面的作业全部完成。

## Q7
- Prediction / 预测: Increasing the RR quantum increases response time while jobs still require multiple time slices. When the quantum is at least as long as every job, RR behaves like FIFO for the given submission order. For N jobs arriving together, the worst-case response time in the first round is (N - 1) * q, provided each preceding job uses a full quantum. For three jobs of length 100, the expected average response times for q = 1, 10, 50, and 100 are 1.00, 10.00, 50.00, and 100.00. / 当作业仍然需要多个时间片才能完成时，增大RR的时间片会使响应时间增加。当时间片不短于所有作业的长度时，RR在给定的提交顺序下会表现得与FIFO相同。对于同时到达的N个作业，如果前面的作业都用满一个时间片，那么第一轮中的最坏响应时间为(N - 1) * q。对于三个长度均为100的作业，当q分别为1、10、50和100时，预计平均响应时间分别为1.00、10.00、50.00和100.00。
- Reasoning / 理由: In RR, a job must wait for the preceding jobs to receive their turns before it can run for the first time. If each preceding job consumes one full quantum, the last job's response time is (N - 1) * q. For three equal-length jobs, the response times are 0, q, and 2q, so their average is q. Once q reaches the job length, each job can finish in a single turn, and RR no longer divides the jobs into multiple slices. / 在RR中，一个作业必须等待排在前面的作业依次获得运行机会，才能首次执行。如果每个前序作业都用满一个时间片，最后一个作业的响应时间就是(N - 1) * q。对于三个等长作业，响应时间分别为0、q和2q，因此平均值为q。当q达到作业长度时，每个作业都能在一次运行中完成，RR不再将它们分割为多个时间片。
- Verified result / 验证结果: For three jobs of length 100, RR with quantum values of 1, 10, 50, and 100 produces average response times of 1.00, 10.00, 50.00, and 100.00, respectively. The corresponding average turnaround times are 299, 290, 250, and 200. / 对于三个长度均为100的作业，RR在时间片分别为1、10、50和100时，平均响应时间依次为1.00、10.00、50.00和100.00；对应的平均周转时间分别为299、290、250和200。
- Analysis / 分析: The simulation agrees with the prediction. For three equal-length jobs, the average response time equals q, while the worst first-response time is 2q. When q reaches 100, each job finishes in its first turn, making RR equivalent to FIFO for this workload. Meanwhile, the average turnaround time decreases as the quantum grows, from 299 to 200, because fewer interruptions occur before completion. Thus, choosing a quantum involves balancing response time against turnaround time. / 模拟结果与预测一致。对于三个等长作业，平均响应时间等于q，而最坏的首次响应时间为2q。当q达到100时，每个作业都能在第一次运行时完成，因此RR在该工作负载下等同于FIFO。同时，随着时间片增大，平均周转时间从299下降到200，这是因为作业在完成前受到的中断更少。因此，时间片的选择需要在响应时间与周转时间之间进行权衡。
